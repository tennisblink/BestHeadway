Each crossover connects two turnouts on different tracks. Both mainline and crossing sides of the same turnout share the same chainage system. A chainage jump between the two turnouts captures the surveying discontinuity between the two mainline chainage systems. Expand each row to enter turnout geometry and the crossing chainage jump.

Turnout A / Turnout B convention: Turnout A is the turnout at the lower chainage side and extends toward increasing chainage; Turnout B is the turnout at the higher chainage side and extends toward decreasing chainage. Turnout A may be on either track. This convention is not enforced; it only sets the direction of the default values below.

Auto-fill: when you enter a turnout chainage, the empty geometry fields of that turnout are filled with defaults (toe = turnout chainage; blade zone = toe ± 30 m; fouling point = blade zone ± 10 m; jump chainage = fouling point ± 10 m; Turnout A adds, Turnout B subtracts). Main and crossing values default to the same number and can be edited separately. The jump tracks always follow the Turnout A / B tracks. Existing values are never overwritten; use Recalculate in the crossover's detail panel to overwrite all fields with the defaults.

Validation: each crossover is checked as you type. Errors (red) and warnings (amber) appear under its row. They do not block saving.
